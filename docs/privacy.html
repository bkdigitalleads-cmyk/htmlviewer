Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

What the app collects

Purchases. If you buy HTML Viewer Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat, the service the app uses to confirm Pro, keeps your purchase history for this app under a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is all. It is the only item on the app's App Store privacy label: Purchases, not linked to you.

What stays on your iPhone

The files you open, the app's copies of them, your edits and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and the app makes no network calls of its own. A file or a PDF leaves your phone only when you share, print or save it somewhere.

Pages from the web

The app does not browse the web. A page you open may point to pictures, styles or scripts on the web; for files from other apps these stay off, so nothing is loaded and the sender cannot see you opened it. If you turn Web content on for a file, that page loads what it asks for from the web, as any page would. Tapping a web link opens it in your browser, outside the app.

What the app does not use

No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. The app asks for one permission, notifications, only to remind you the day before a free trial ends, and only if you start one. Paste code uses the system paste button, so the app never reads your clipboard on its own.
